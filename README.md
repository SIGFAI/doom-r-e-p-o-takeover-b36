# R.E.P.O. Takeover

**Doom** × R.E.P.O. · visual · idea by the team

Bring R.E.P.O. into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> Doom, but it's R.E.P.O.: gnomes and a mutant duck raid you while you grab loot and try not to shatter the vases.

## Install

Put mod.pk3 next to GZDoom and run: gzdoom -iwad freedoom2.wad -file mod.pk3 (any Doom II IWAD works). Grab diamonds and gold bars to fill the quota, and avoid breaking vases.
